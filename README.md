## 🚀 Overview

**MATSEL PRO** is an AI-assisted material selection and product-design intelligence platform that integrates:

- Engineering properties
- Surface finish characteristics
- Product aesthetics
- Manufacturing processes
- Cost estimation
- Sustainability considerations
- Industry/application constraints
- AI-based visual analysis

The platform was developed to address a fundamental limitation in conventional material-selection workflows:

> Traditional engineering material selection primarily focuses on mechanical, thermal, chemical and economic properties, while aesthetic requirements such as surface texture, gloss, perceived warmth, visual language and emotional response are often handled separately and subjectively.

MATSEL PRO attempts to bridge this gap by converting aesthetic design intent into measurable engineering parameters and using them to support material and process selection.

The system combines a structured material database with a rule-based recommendation engine, manufacturing-process intelligence, parametric cost estimation and an AI-powered vision module.
